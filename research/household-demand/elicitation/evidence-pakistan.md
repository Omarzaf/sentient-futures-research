# Pakistan public evidence pack

Frozen for elicitation on 19 October 2026. Public-source observations and named external estimates are separated below. No project market forecast, fitted household coefficient, internal price ratio, scenario result or microdata is supplied. Raw captures remain private; public access does not establish redistribution rights.

## Household consumption context

PBS's public HIES 2024-25 report describes conventional household expenditure and food quantities. It does not identify a comprehensive national retail series for plant-based replacement products. The report's fieldwork end dates differ between foreword and preface, and its food summary differs from a separately linked Table 23 workbook. These are source-vintage limitations; this pack supplies no reconciled project estimate or unit record. [P1]

## Trade proxy

A public UN Comtrade preview captured on 16 October 2026 reports Pakistan's 2025 world-partner imports under HS 210610 as USD 2,064,737.24 and net weight 1,276,672 kg. The row is marked aggregate, not directly reported. This is upstream trade in protein concentrates and textured protein substances, not domestic alternative-protein retail turnover. It includes uses outside this questionnaire's market definition; do not infer sales, consumption, or market size from it. Future revisions and missing observations remain possible. [P2]

## Consumer evidence and regional comparison

Bryant et al.'s 2019 online sample covers India, the US and China, not Pakistan. India reported 62.8% very/extremely likely to buy plant-based meat and the US 32.9%, with 1,024 and 987 respondents respectively. India's sample skewed urban, higher-income and educated. These are stated intent, not paid trial or repeat purchases. Transferring either share to Pakistan is a forecasting assumption, not a Pakistan observation. No Pakistan-specific representative conversion estimate is supplied. [P3]

## External market estimate and regional policy

GFI India / Deloitte's 2022 analysis estimated India's domestic smart-protein market at INR 351 crore across meat, dairy and eggs. It contains India-only 2030 scenarios and no Pakistan market estimate. This source is relevant only as an explicitly disclosed regional comparison and the India republication proxy. Its model outputs must not become a Pakistan observed baseline. [P4]

India's BioE3 policy announcement is regional policy context, not Pakistan approval or Pakistan demand evidence. A first-sale claim in Pakistan needs evidence from the competent food authorities plus an actual legal paid sale; halal compliance is assumed for the question and does not by itself establish food authorization. [P5]

## Missing evidence

No complete Pakistan retail-sales series, comprehensive product-count baseline, representative repeated-purchase study or measured country-specific AI cost decline is included. Do not equate missing observations with zero. The questionnaire headline is unresolvable now; trade, listing counts and an Indian external-estimate republication are separate proxies.
