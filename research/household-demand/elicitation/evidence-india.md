# India public evidence pack

Frozen for elicitation on 19 October 2026. The facts below are public-source observations or explicitly named external estimates. No household-model result, project market forecast, internal price ratio or microdata is supplied. Public access does not establish republication rights; linked originals retain their own terms. These are short factual summaries, not redistributed source documents.

## Market scope and external estimates

GFI India and Deloitte's 2022 Smart Protein Economic Analysis, final-page domestic-market table, estimated the 2022 Indian market at INR 351 crore: meat 100 crore, dairy 250 crore and eggs 1 crore. These are historical modeled estimates, not audited retail sales or constant-2025-dollar observations. Its 2030 scenarios combine plant-based meat, dairy, eggs and fermentation-derived meat. They are named external forecasts and have broader technology scope than this questionnaire. The source's proprietary model and expert consultation do not make its scenarios probabilities. Any use must be disclosed as potential anchoring. [S1]

## Household consumption context

MoSPI's public NSS Report 594 covers nutritional intake from HCES 2022-23 and 2023-24. It describes conventional household food consumption; these tables do not measure national plant-based replacement-product purchases or repeated paid adoption. No household records or project reconstruction is included. [S2]

## Stated intent and its limits

Bryant and colleagues' 2019 online study used the same questionnaire in the US and India. Very/extremely likely to buy plant-based meat was 62.8% in India and 32.9% in the US; sample sizes were 1,024 and 987. The Indian sample skewed urban, affluent and educated. These are stated-intent shares, not representative purchase penetration or causal responses to prices. It supplies no corresponding dairy-alternative intent measure and no Pakistan sample. The article's later corrigendum concerns clean-meat figures and author spelling, not these plant-based shares. [S3]

## Trade proxy

The retained UN Comtrade preview request for India, reporter 356, reference year 2025, world partner 0, flow M, including HS 210610, returned an empty data array on 16 October 2026 after an earlier rate-limit response. This means unavailable in this capture, not zero imports. HS 210610 includes upstream protein concentrates and textured protein substances, so even a complete observation would not be retail alternative-protein sales. [S4]

## Policy and regulatory evidence

The official 24 August 2024 BioE3 announcement includes high-performance biomanufacturing and smart proteins. The 11 March 2026 parliamentary response describes biomanufacturing platforms. Policy support and multipurpose equipment do not establish product safety approval, commercial output or paid demand. Seek current FSSAI evidence for any claim about permission to sell cultivated meat. [S5, S6]

## Missing evidence

This pack contains no complete national retail series, representative trial-to-repeat conversion estimate, verified future product-count series or country-specific AI cost attribution. Proxies retain their own denominators and must not be presented as headline demand. Source retrieval dates and capture hashes are in source-provenance.json; some records rely on the source catalog's earlier review rather than a fresh October 19 retrieval.
