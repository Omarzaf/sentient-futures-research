# Protein Transitions Across Unequal Food Systems

[Open the complete illustrated report](index.html). Evidence cutoff: 13 September 2026. Earlier comparative background synthesis; AI-assisted draft with human verification pending.

The report covers protein supply, adoption and substitution, public research and talent policy, institutional capabilities, and conditional transition scenarios. It is broader than the current India–Pakistan scope and is not a completed causal study.

## Evidence files

- [Original source register](source-register.json): 60 records with stable report IDs and access limitations.
- [Cited-passage ledger](claim-ledger.json): reviewable passages and source links; pending review is preserved.
- [Country data](protein-data.csv): 13 selected cases, including one missing-data case.
- [Figure inputs and provenance](figure-data.json): retained source rows, page locators, calculations, and 14 capability locations.
- [Supply chart](protein-supply-2023.svg) and [share-change chart](protein-share-change.svg).
- [Country locators](comparison-map.svg) and [capability locators](capability-map.svg).

Food supply is not individual intake. The locations do not measure employment. Pakistan is not included in this extracted country dataset. The report's original photographs and raster background are omitted in this repository edition; credits and original-source records remain available.
