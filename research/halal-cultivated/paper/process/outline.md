## Thesis — 103 words

Halal market access for cultivated chicken depends on a specified product and process, a named religious authority, and distinct certification and food authorization decisions. The audited sources support conditional routes and competing interpretations, but they do not supply a single rule shared across the five traditions examined, a certificate for a named product, or an access forecast. Donor procurement, culture inputs, transformation and edibility therefore need separate analysis before institutional and regulatory findings can be combined. The paper’s contribution is a traceable comparison along the production chain that preserves disagreement and missing evidence, and states what a product dossier would need to resolve.
