# China: African swine fever and the poultry comparison

**This documents a conventional livestock shock and simultaneous changes in poultry production and household purchases.** Causality and persistence require separate evidence. It does not supply a cultivated-chicken adoption rate. The [structured case](china-asf-history.json) links the observations, source versions and unknowns to original requirement HISTORY-18.

## Production changed sharply

| NBS annual output, million tons as published | 2018 | 2019 | Difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Pork | 54.04 | 42.55 | −11.49 |
| Poultry | 19.94 | 22.39 | +2.45 |

The release for 2019, published on 28 February 2020, reports changes of −21.3% and +12.3%, respectively. Both releases label their statistics preliminary and exclude Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan. These are production quantities; poultry is broader than chicken, and food mass is not nutrient-protein mass. **Calendar 2018 already includes the outbreak's first months**, so it is not an untreated baseline. [NBS 2018 release](https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/201902/t20190228_1651335.html), [NBS 2019 release](https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202002/t20200228_1728917.html).

## Household series rebounded; permanence is unestablished

ERS reproduces NBS household pork purchases of 22.8 kg per person in 2018, 20.3 in 2019, 18.2 in 2020 and 25.8 in 2021. NBS's own 2021 figure is widely reported as 25.2 kg (its 2022 figure, 26.9 kg up 6.75%, implies 25.2), so the 2021 value needs checking against the NBS yearbook before reuse. Its Table 4 reports poultry rising 3.7 kg per person during 2018–2020, then falling 0.4 kg in 2020–2021. The series excludes meals away from home, and ERS flags possible survey or pandemic-related composition effects in 2021. These are kilograms of food, not protein. Table 4's printed reference to Figure 11 differs from the adjacent Figure 10; the original discrepancy is preserved. [Gale, Kee and Huang, ERR-326, pp. 22–23](https://ers.usda.gov/sites/default/files/_laserfiche/publications/107925/ERR-326.pdf).

## Mechanism and institutional response

FAO's contemporary account describes disease zoning, live-pig movement controls, affected-market closures and a swill-feeding ban. It dates confirmation of the Liaoning outbreak to 3 August 2018; ERS dates the initial report to 1 August. Both support an August 2018 start, while exact-day reconciliation remains open. These controls and the supply disruption are documented; their separate effects on poultry purchases are not identified here. [FAO, 1 March 2019](https://www.fao.org/myanmar/news/detail-events/en/c/1177347/), [ERS, p. 4](https://ers.usda.gov/sites/default/files/_laserfiche/publications/107925/ERR-326.pdf).

Hao and coauthors' publisher abstract reports a 2015–2020, 31-province difference-in-differences study and heterogeneous pork/poultry responses. The publisher identifies the article as 2023, whereas ERS cites 2022. The abstract-level account is retained; full identification assumptions, diagnostics and coefficients have not been assessed. [Original publisher record](https://nyxdhyj.isa.ac.cn/article/doi/10.13872/j.1000-0275.2023.0017?viewType=citedby-info).

Ma and coauthors' [2021 publisher excerpts](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0306919221000427) describe inverse-demand estimation and subsidy simulations. The final full text was not obtained; an accessible-index lead to a 2020 conference version returned no usable document. No elasticity or simulated subsidy result is imported. A ratio of annual output changes would not identify a demand elasticity or the fraction of consumers who switched.

## Relevance to the forecast

The useful analogy is a testable question: can an existing substitute's supply expand at an acceptable price when the incumbent contracts, and does the change persist after recovery? For cultivated chicken, capacity, affordability and persistence need their own evidence. China's pork-centred disease episode supplies neither a religious gate nor a numerical penetration or capacity input for India, Pakistan or other countries. The case can inform hypothesis design while those inputs remain unknown.
