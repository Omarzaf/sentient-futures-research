**India now has a named administrative record to follow.** FSSAI's September 2026 register places Biokraft Foods' cultivated chicken-based product in its Under Process section, row 28, with stage 7. The legend groups clarification, approval and rejection under that stage. The receipt date is 20 August 2025; it is not an approval date. The underlying decision remains needed. [Register, PDF pages 64, 66 and 79](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/standards/specfic-food/Status%20of%20application%20as%20on%2001.09.2026.pdf).

**India's halal export conditions require a product and destination match.** The 2024 notification is limited to specified tariff codes and halal-certified exports. The February 2026 amendment adds twenty destinations. The August amendment extends Egypt's transition to nine months from 9 February 2026. These instruments do not establish domestic cultivated-chicken approval or its tariff classification. [2024 conditions](https://apeda.gov.in/sites/default/files/dgft_notifications/Notification_No_01_10_2024.pdf), [February amendment](https://apeda.gov.in/sites/default/files/dgft_notifications/Notification_59.pdf), [August amendment](https://apeda.gov.in/sites/default/files/dgft_notifications/28_2026_27_Notification.pdf).

**Pakistan's institutional account needs the newer constitutional edition.** Articles 227–230 distinguish personal-law interpretation, Council membership and advisory functions. The 2025 edition uses a one-fourth referral threshold and includes a twelve-month final-report consideration requirement. The 2017 edition also contains a printed cross-reference discrepancy. Neither edition measures population affiliation. [Fourteenth Edition, printed pages 139–141](https://na.gov.pk/uploads/documents/6926e060076ed_467.pdf), [2017 edition, printed page 136](https://www.na.gov.pk/uploads/documents/1515056252_668.pdf).

**Punjab supplies a concrete provincial registration procedure.** Its regulator lists the documents and review stages, including raw-material halal certificates. This is generic guidance, not a cultivated-food decision or a nationwide route. [Punjab Food Authority guidance](https://pfa.gop.pk/product-registration/).

**Malaysia's legal-method rule is territorial and dated.** The Federal Territories Act's 2013 reprint sets a Shafii default with specified alternatives and gazetting requirements. It cannot establish national population prevalence or the legal effect of every national committee opinion. [Act 505, sections 34, 37 and 39](https://muftiwp.gov.my/images/archive/akta_pekeliling/Act_505-Admistration_of_Islamic_Law__Federal_Territories_Act_1993.pdf).
