# GOOD Meat: a named cultivated-chicken process

**AI-assisted working account; human review pending.** This dossier describes the U.S. process reviewed under FDA consultation CCC000001 in 2022–2023. It is a concrete input for technical and religious questions, not a finding that a product is halal or currently authorized everywhere. The [structured dossier](manufacturing-dossier.json) preserves stage details, measurements and unresolved fields.

## Identity and version

The [company submission](https://www.fda.gov/media/166346/download) and [FDA assessment](https://www.fda.gov/media/166348/download) describe fibroblast-characteristic chicken cells, derived from the UMNSAH/DF1 line (ATCC CRL12203), then named C1F and adapted to C1F-P1. The parental line came from ten-day chicken embryos. This does not supply evidence of an adult donor's slaughter, embryo-specific halal procurement or a current retail product's identity. The documented culture facility is JOINN Biologics US in Richmond, California; its current status is unestablished here. [Submission §§4.1–4.6; assessment pp. 4–6](https://www.fda.gov/media/166348/download).

The March 2022 narrative must be read with four amendments received through March 2023. One reduces folic acid from 2.65 to 0.265 mg/L, while retaining the banks and manufacturing method otherwise. Its cover and signature dates differ; the crosswalk preserves both. The change does not remove fetal bovine serum. [Submission, PDF pp. 135 and 140–145](https://www.fda.gov/media/166346/download).

Suspension culture alone does not prove the absence of scaffolds or animal-derived components in every later food. ## Measurements that change the question

Table 33 reports bovine serum albumin in four harvested-cell batches: **42.46, 11.97, 5.27 and 3.0 µg/g**. Its reported mean is 15.67 µg/g, or approximately 1.57 mg/100 g. The displayed values average 15.675; the register preserves the source's reported rounding. BSA is one measured analyte, not a measurement of all serum constituents or a religious purity determination. [Submission p. 60](https://www.fda.gov/media/166346/download), [FDA assessment p. 20](https://www.fda.gov/media/166348/download).

Final-wash liquid results use **mg/L** and must remain separate from cell-material results. Values below a detection limit are not zero. Table 45's six harvested-cell batches contain 7.75–9.75% protein and 88.73–90.54% moisture on a wet basis. Neither a dry-normalized comparison nor the intended ingredient share supplies the nutritional composition of an actual retail serving. [Submission pp. 64, 73 and 75](https://www.fda.gov/media/166346/download).

## Regulatory and religious records

FDA's [response](https://www.fda.gov/media/166347/download) raises no questions about the firm's safety conclusion for the defined process; the [agency's announcement](https://www.fda.gov/food/hfp-constituent-updates/fda-completes-second-pre-market-consultation-human-food-made-using-animal-cell-culture-technology) explicitly distinguishes consultation from approval. [June correspondence](https://www.fda.gov/media/169702/download) corrects the labeling-authority account: USDA-FSIS responsibility extends from harvest. These documents do not establish a USDA inspection grant or halal certificate.

Singapore's serum-free-media approval is a **separate record**, supported by a [company announcement](https://www.goodmeat.co/all-news/good-meat-receives-approval-to-commercialize-serum-free-media) and [ministerial speech, paragraph 21](https://www.mse.gov.sg/latest-news/speech-by-sms-koh-poh-koon-at-cos-2023/). The actual SFA instrument, complete formula and bank-history match were not obtained. A serum-free medium does not establish animal-component-free provenance throughout the process.

A [participant account of 2023 scholarly advice](https://www.eversheds-sutherland.bg/en/saudi-arabia/news/al-dhabaan-partners-in-association-with-eversheds-sutherland-advises-good-meat-on-the-acceptance-of) is not a certifier-issued document. Certificate number, validity, product, site, bank and medium match all remain unknown. The next interpretation should therefore examine this exact donor–input–residue chain and request the missing provenance, without converting consultation, washing or advice into certification.
