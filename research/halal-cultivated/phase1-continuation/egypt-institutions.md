## Al-Azhar-related statement

The World Organization for Al-Azhar Graduates published a [24 September 2024 report quoting Abbas Shoman](https://waag-azhar.org/34362), identified there as the organization's chair and secretary-general of the Council of Senior Scholars. The complete short report was returned by the search provider; direct retrieval failed.

Our provisional reading attributes to Shoman a slaughtered-donor restriction, exclusion of cells taken from a living donor with a fish/locust exception, nutrients free from ritual impurities, and demonstrated absence of health harm. The exact exception's scope remains for qualified Arabic and jurisprudential review. “Living donor” describes procurement; it must not be rewritten as a ban on viable cultured cells. [WAAG, first paragraph](https://waag-azhar.org/34362).

The report links the statement to a Jeddah seminar paper and asserts agreement with the Council's approval. That is an attributed report of concurrence. The full paper, original Council resolution, decision date, number and operative scope were not retrieved. The graduates' organization, a university department, the Council and a fatwa office must remain distinct. [WAAG, final paragraph](https://waag-azhar.org/34362).

## Dar al-Ifta Egypt

A [Veto article dated 3 September 2023](https://www.vetogate.com/4965870) reproduces a conditional answer attributed to Dar al-Ifta: an edible slaughtered donor, no living-donor biopsy, exclusion of impure cultivation inputs, and absence of immediate or future health harm, alongside food-safety requirements. This is a secondary reproduction. Its news date is not the original fatwa date; the original author, number and full institutional page remain unverified.

The reproduced explanation's technical premises must also be assessed separately. A description that treats cultivated meat as genetically modified and serum-dependent cannot be generalized to every process. The reproduction is retained as a useful locator and an attributed account, not an accepted original institutional ruling. [Veto, explanation and conditions](https://www.vetogate.com/4965870).
