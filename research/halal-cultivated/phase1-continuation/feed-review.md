## Economic question

Observed annual value of insect-derived meal sold as animal-feed input within each country; product/year/currency/coverage must be explicit. This packet does not substitute production, capacity, project output or all-feed market totals.

Diet ingredient price, formulated-feed cost per kg, feed intake, FCR (feed per bodyweight gain), feed cost per kg gain, total farm production cost and retail chicken price are different outcomes. As an accounting identity for compatible units, feed cost per kg liveweight gain equals formulated-feed cost per kg multiplied by FCR. Other farm costs, yield, mortality, processing, transport, regulation and margins affect chicken cost. This identity supplies no parameter or observed causal effect.

## Religious assessment: five named-school routes

Input species, purity, substrate, live/dead state, processing, mixture and eventual poultry status are separate questions. A human-food insect rule cannot automatically decide poultry-feed status. The named passages below do not constitute a school consensus or product certificate.

### Hanafi

Garden live-worm question; no inspected industrial insect-meal ruling. Quoted mixed-feed/changed-flesh distinction; original cited edition unverified. No product-specific determination.

The mirrored Askimam answer tells the questioner there is no need to feed live worms because chickens hunt them themselves. Its supporting quotation distinguishes mixed feeding from jallala with altered flesh. The answer does not give an industrial insect-meal approval. [R7-F-ASK125569](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/125569/live-worms-is-manure-impure/). Original issuer page, Deoband6945 and cited print edition remain unacquired. Attribution is to this answer, not all Hanafi authorities.

Reopen when: Retrieve issuer answer/Deoband6945 and exact Tuhfat al-fuqaha3:65, then assess a defined formulation.

### Maliki

No directly inspected Maliki insect-feed instrument in this run. Later institutional attribution and a Dasuqi locator are leads, not an adopted school rule. Unknown for a defined insect-meal formulation.

### Shafii

Issuer separates live/dead insects and mixture composition; its answer is conditional. Classical smell/taint discussion cannot become a universal insect-inclusion cutoff. A named formulation, harm evidence and qualified interpretation remain necessary.

Jordan’s General Iftaa Department distinguishes live worms/insects, dead insects and feed mixtures, reports a Shafii qualification, and conditions its answer on expert assessment of harm. This is an attributed institutional answer, not certification of a named insect-meal product. [R7-F-JOR4015](https://www.aliftaa.jo/QuestionPrint.aspx?QuestionId=4015). Arabic interpretation pending qualified review; quoted classical passages are not all independently acquired. No blanket school consensus, national market permission or product safety finding.

The indexed al-Majmu discussion contrasts a feed-majority criterion with a smell/taint criterion. It therefore cannot be reduced to a universal percentage of insect inclusion. [R7-F-MAJMU-JALLALA](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/14/5286/حيوان-البحر-يحل-منه-السمك). Exact print edition and complete passage require checking. ## Human insect collection and eating: bounded South Asian account

Field studies in Arunachal Pradesh document insect collection and eating among particular communities: Nyishi and Galo respondents in East Kameng and West Siang in a study published in 2011, and six groups in Tirap and Changlang in a study published in 2013. Each study interviewed 20 informants per group. The former explicitly describes its evidence as qualitative; both concern situated knowledge and uses rather than representative consumption prevalence. The studies share an author and research programme. These accounts do not describe all South Asia, establish medicinal efficacy, or provide human-protein demand totals; publication years are not established fieldwork dates. [R7-F-ETH2011](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1746-4269-7-5), [R7-F-ETH2013](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1746-4269-9-50).
