## Saudi Arabia

The legal route exists and has halal built in, but no cultivated product is approved. The religious answer turns on donor procurement: Saudi scholars advising GOOD Meat require a slaughtered donor, while IIFA, whose seminar the SFDA co-organised, keeps a living-donor route.

## United Arab Emirates

The weakest focal country on religious evidence. A slaughtered-donor position by the UAE Council for Fatwa is reported only by a tertiary source and was not confirmed. On the legal side, the UAE told Codex that cell-based food falls under the federal novel-food standard UAE.S 5048:2021, which requires pre-market evaluation; no application or approval was found, and Abu Dhabi's single-window framework was still a proposal in January 2026.

## India

The only focal country with a named cultivated-chicken application, still under process at FSSAI, though approval of novel ingredients needs a Food Authority that had not been reconstituted as of January 2026. No Indian Islamic institution's ruling was located. Non-halal chicken is legal, so the religious gate is legally a segment; traders estimate that about 90% of meat sold is halal, so in practice it may cover most of the market.

## Reading the labels

- Accepted if conditions met: Retained positions accept this profile if every stated condition is met.
- Disputed: Retained authorities with standing in this country disagree.
- Leans against: The only retained position for this country rejects the profile, from a weak source.
- Conditions not met: The documented process fails at least one condition of the retained positions.
- Unresolved: No retained position classifies this founder-cell type.
- No ruling found: No ruling from a relevant institution in this country was located.
- General system, no cultivated scheme: A general halal certification system exists; no scheme for cell-cultured products was found.
- Private and contested: Halal certification is private and politically contested for domestic sale.
- Route exists, no approval: A food-authorization route covering cell culture exists; no approval was found.
- Application pending: A named application is under process.
- Announced, status unknown: A route has been announced; whether it operates is unknown.
- No route found: No authorization route for cultivated meat was identified.
- Whole market: Halal status conditions sale across the market.
- Segment only: Halal status conditions sale only to a segment of buyers.
- Not established: Not established.

Confidence: low rests on secondary or snippet-level sources or on inference alone; medium rests on at least one checked source plus consistent leads. No cell is rated high.

Claim IDs starting P1C8 are 2 October search leads or labelled inferences (status `inference`). Leads read against their original text on 2 October have status `confirmed_original`; the rest stay `open`, and one is `disputed`. IDs starting P1C9 were added during that check.
