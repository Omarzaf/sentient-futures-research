## HP-14

**United States margarine and butter**

USDA ERS reports butter availability falling from 16.4 to 5.0 lb/person/year between 1942 and 1972. Margarine crossed butter in 1958, peaked at 12.0 lb in 1976 and fell below butter by 2005 despite remaining cheaper. These are availability proxies, not causal replacement measurements. Scarcity, soybean supply, policy, formulation and health perceptions moved together. The reversal makes a permanent technology-driven displacement assumption especially weak.

United States; 1942–1976 expansion; reversal by 2005
