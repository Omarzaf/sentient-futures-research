# Cultivated chicken in India, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and the UAE: where the evidence stands

2 October 2026, updated after checking the retained leads against originals. Draft for mentor review. AI-assisted; human verification pending, and no claim has had human scholarly review. This page summarises the [October continuation](README.md) of the [30 September Phase One review](../phase1/review.md). The country-by-country detail is in the [gate table](gate-table.md).

## The short answer

No cultivated-chicken approval in a focal country is established by the packet inspected through 2 October 2026. That is a bounded evidence gap, not proof that no approval exists. India has a named administrative record whose actual decision is unresolved. The UAE government describes a route covering cell-based food; the complete standard remains unread. Saudi instrument versions and operative coverage need reconciliation, and no applicable Pakistani cultivated-food route was located in this packet. Reported conditional religious positions do not establish national adoption, universal consensus or product certification.

- Saudi Arabia: The current food-authorization and certification scope is unverified. Earlier SFDA material and an unconfirmed USDA summary suggest requirements relevant to animal-origin novel foods, but they do not establish whole-market coverage. The SFDA co-organised the September 2024 seminar cited by IIFA Resolution 265; participation does not make IIFA’s international resolution a Saudi national ruling. Manufacturer and counsel accounts report that three named Saudi scholars advising GOOD Meat required a slaughtered donor among other conditions. IIFA’s retained text also permits a living-donor route, within its own conditions and scope.
- United Arab Emirates: In a Codex submission, the UAE stated that cell-based food falls under UAE.S 5048:2021 and requires pre-market evaluation. This supports an attributed government account, not a reading of the standard or a product approval. Abu Dhabi’s announced single-window framework was described as a proposal by a law firm in January 2026; later status is unknown. A position attributed to the UAE Council for Fatwa remains an unconfirmed tertiary report. Its religious cells are unresolved.
- Pakistan: No applicable cultivated-food authorization route was identified in this bounded packet. Punjab supplies generic provincial registration guidance. A 2022 Bloomberg report attributes a slaughtered-donor requirement to scholars led by Mufti Muhammad Taqi Usmani; the original ruling is unlocated. The Banuri Town Urdu answer remains held for qualified language review. These sources do not establish acceptance or rejection of the six profiles.
- India: FSSAI’s September 2026 register places Biokraft Foods’ cultivated chicken-based product under process at stage 7, whose legend combines clarification, approval and rejection. The actual decision is needed. A January 2026 report describes a Food Authority lapse; its later status is unknown. No applicable Indian institutional ruling was located in this packet. Specified export instruments and a news-reported state-order meat exclusion do not establish comprehensive domestic legal coverage. Traders’ unofficial estimate that about 90% of all meat sold is halal measures neither chicken-specific supply nor purchases contingent on halal status; it cannot set a demand-segment share.

## Founder cells are one unresolved condition

The retained sources attach conditions to species, procurement, inputs and other process or safety matters, but they differ in scope and verification state. Their positions cannot be counted as a universal vote. The [institution matrix](institution-matrix.md) preserves the seven-condition comparison and its unknowns.

| Founder route | What the retained evidence supports | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Slaughtered donor | A conditional route in IIFA 265 and the manufacturer/counsel account of GOOD Meat’s advisers | All stated conditions still apply. Unconfirmed reports, held-language material and silence do not establish wider agreement; no national adoption or matched certificate follows |
| Living-donor biopsy | IIFA’s retained text includes this alternative; the reported GOOD Meat adviser account requires slaughter | Different scope and wording do not establish a formal Saudi institutional dispute. The American Halal Foundation’s retained pages also differ |
| Egg- or embryo-derived line | A procurement question raised by retained manufacturing examples; Hamdan supplies an author argument, and the MUIS passages remain unreconciled | Neither an author argument nor an unreconciled exception is an applicable focal-country product ruling |
| Feather-derived reprogrammed cells | The retained American Fiqh Academy text discusses this route | No focal-country adoption or matching product is established in this packet |

Wifaq ul Ulama’s UK page reproduces the American Fiqh Academy text, so the two are one source family, not independent corroboration. The held Nahdlatul Ulama account’s rejection of living-donor cells cannot establish acceptance of another procurement route.

The examples are process-specific. GOOD Meat’s FDA-reviewed CCC000001 dossier documents fibroblasts derived from 10-day chicken embryos and fetal bovine serum. It does not establish equivalence with Singapore’s 2020 approval or the later serum-free version. The later Singapore announcement does not establish a founder-line or cell-bank match to the FDA dossier. The retained SFA list describes Vital Meat’s embryonic-stem-cell process and a medium without animal-derived components. These are retained examples, not an inventory of every existing cultivated-chicken product. An FDA consultation or food-safety listing is not a halal certificate.

GOOD Meat reported in September 2023 that its then line and process did not meet the advisers’ criteria. The exact failed condition and its match to CCC000001 remain unestablished. Donor and serum questions therefore remain hypotheses for a matched-process review, not confirmed P1 failure findings or current nonconformity. A founder-cell classification would resolve only one part of a full product assessment.

An unconfirmed Kuwaiti report describes the earlier Jeddah seminar as requiring a slaughtered donor, while the retained final IIFA resolution includes a living-donor alternative. Without the seminar recommendations themselves, this cannot establish a change in drafting or explain the apparent difference.

## What the evidence can carry

- The continuation register holds 188 sources and 156 claims, including the provenance-preserving SFA-list copy from the prior register. Of the claims, 129 had a second AI read of the same retrieved source, 10 were read against the original document on 2 October, 11 remain unconfirmed leads, 1 is disputed, and 5 are labelled inferences. The five inferences have been narrowed without promoting their status.
- No claim has had human review. “Independently checked” tests transcription and reading, not whether the correct source was found. “Confirmed against original” means the coordinator read the source’s own text; for a news report it confirms the report, not the instrument behind it.
- Access failures limited retrieval. Some originals were read through a document index; a source’s presence in an index does not establish complete current law, qualified translation or independent corroboration.
- Urdu, Malay and Indonesian texts remain held under the English-first rule, including the Banuri Town answer, the Nahdlatul Ulama account and Malaysia’s 2025 national fatwa council opinion. No table reading overrides those holds.

A January 2026 article on thenationmiddleeast.com was rejected as unreliable. The American Halal Foundation’s living-donor accounts remain marked disputed.

## The supporting work

- Demand elasticities: The 186 retained study-level estimates include mismatched species and aggregate categories, conditioning differences and source anomalies. None is accepted as a current cultivated-meat forecast parameter or a direct cultivated/conventional-chicken substitution estimate. Any alternative model or meta-analysis would require its own source and applicability review before use as a stated prior.
- Insects as poultry feed: No national market value or industry chicken-cost effect was established by the four inspected country packets. IIFA 267 postpones its insect ruling. Ingredient legality remains a separate unresolved question.
- Historical parallels: The 21 cases illustrate mechanisms, not identified adoption speeds. The rennet and insulin cases can guide further comparison, but dates alone would not identify a transferable causal effect or forecast rate.
- Muslim geography: Dated affiliation estimates and constitutional texts provide context. They do not set gates, and juristic-school shares remain unmeasured.
