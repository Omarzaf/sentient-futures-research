# D2. AI in the driver projections

Draft date: 2026-10-17. AI-assisted draft; human verification pending.

AI could help alternative proteins and conventional animal production. Its net market effect remains unresolved. This row supplies the evidence, three speculative directions and a signpost for "Projecting the drivers forward" in `research/india-pakistan/report/REPORT.md`.

## Driver projection row

| Driver, projection and signpost |
| --- |
| AI. Evidence: Media optimisation and protein design have experimental evidence. Cell Forge advertises AI and fermentation; BioE3 provides for Bio-AI hubs. Older reports link Cowlar and Poulta to Pakistani livestock. [2026]{.allow-number data-number-kind="year"}: Establish baselines in both countries. [2030]{.allow-number data-number-kind="year"}: Indian alternative proteins and Pakistani dairy and poultry could improve if pilots validate gains. [2035]{.allow-number data-number-kind="year"}: Relative competitiveness depends on which sector reduces costs faster. Signpost: Independently checked AI versus non-AI yield, cost per usable protein output and retail price gaps. |

## Supporting text

AI belongs in the comparison as a possible change in relative production costs. Cosenza et al. used Bayesian optimisation to search culture media. Watson et al. experimentally tested designed proteins. Neither establishes cheaper food in India or Pakistan.[^S1][^S2] The background review distinguishes AlphaFold structure prediction from food manufacturing; protein design still needs expression, safety and functionality tests.[^S0]

In India, Cell Forge describes an AI-based platform for recombinant proteins and precision fermentation. This is a company claim, not independently verified performance.[^S3] BioE3 provides for Bio-AI hubs and biomanufacturing facilities. The Smart Proteins call explicitly includes AI monitoring and predictive control of fermentation.[^S4][^S5] These provisions do not establish delivery, food approval or retail sales.

Older Cowlar pages describe machine learning for cow monitoring; current activity is unverified.[^S6] Poulta advertises poultry optimisation, but its homepage lists only US and Canada addresses. Its Pakistan link rests on an undated SUN Business Network programme case study pointing to about 2021.[^S7][^S8] These reports do not isolate AI's effects. US precision-dairy evidence is a comparator, not a transferable effect size.[^S9]

The projections are speculative directions. For 2026, establish baselines; by 2030, check repeatable pilot results; by 2035, compare relative costs. The same tests should apply to the US comparison. Add no separate AI uplift to the market scenarios. Any later quantified effect must account for overlap with price, policy and industry activity. Independently validated country-level AI effects on alternative-protein market size were not found in the sources searched.
