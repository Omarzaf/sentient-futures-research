## Method

The calculation anchors India to GFI India's [2022]{.allow-number data-number-kind="year"} domestic estimate of [351]{.claim data-claim="proxyin_gfi_india_domestic_2022_central"} crore rupees. Inflation and currency conversion put it at [45]{.claim data-claim="proxy_india_2022_anchor_market_value_value"} million dollars. India's later spending per person scales with real income, urbanisation and annual drift for price, taste and availability. Pakistan's spending scales India's by relative income, urban share, animal-protein supply and industry activity. IMF population converts spending per person to totals. Independent uniform draws produce lower, middle and upper scenarios. Proxy selection, functional form and distributions are analyst choices. These are speculative scenarios without tested calibration.

Definitions remain proposed: market-exchange-rate dollars, constant [2025]{.allow-number data-number-kind="year"} prices, retail value, and domestic plant-based meat, dairy, egg alternatives and fermentation-derived foods. Cultivated meat enters only after authorisation. US coverage includes prepared meals. GFI India does not specify retail valuation or nominal rupees; both are assumed. Pakistan has no domestic market observation. A verified sales total would matter more than further proxy refinement.

AI evidence from [Cosenza et al.](<https://doi.org/10.1002/elsc.202300005>) and [Watson et al.](<https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06415-8>) supports media optimisation and experimental protein design, without establishing cheaper regional food. Cell Forge advertises AI and fermentation; BioE3 provides policy support. Cowlar's current activity is unverified. Poulta's Pakistan link rests on older programme reporting. Neither establishes an isolated AI effect. Compare validated yields, usable-protein costs and retail prices across alternative proteins and conventional dairy and poultry. Add no separate AI uplift; effects may overlap price, policy and industry activity.

## Talent and allocation

AltProtein.Jobs records [529]{.claim data-claim="jobs_vacancies_india_2025"} Indian and [58]{.claim data-claim="jobs_vacancies_pakistan_2025"} Pakistani vacancies in [2025]{.allow-number data-number-kind="year"}. These are job-board demand signals, not employment or measured shortages. The workbook has no role or function columns. Business categories cannot establish occupational demand.

As an allocation proposal, not an estimated ranking of returns, fund employer-linked technical trials in India and a small ingredient-application pilot in Pakistan. India's employers should nominate a constraint and compare training with shared facilities. Pakistan's Shafi Gluco Chem directory entries may overlap. Confirm facilities and staff before equipment funding. Test ingredient specifications, safety documentation and formulation. Neither the AltProtein.Jobs vacancy workbook (represented in altprotein_jobs.csv) nor the AI and the Protein Transition literature review measures time to hire or build capacity. Compare baseline and completion results before expansion. Computational funding requires evidence that computation, data or experimental design limits progress, plus experimental validation. Do not add vacancies as another growth multiplier. Anna's talent cruxes remain pending.

GFI India's [2030]{.allow-number data-number-kind="year"} domestic scenarios convert to [964]{.claim data-claim="proxy_india_2030_gfi_india_domestic_scenario_low"}, [1649]{.claim data-claim="proxy_india_2030_gfi_india_domestic_scenario_central"} and [2447]{.claim data-claim="proxy_india_2030_gfi_india_domestic_scenario_high"} million dollars on the same assumptions. [100]{.claim data-claim="proxy_india_2030_draws_below_gfi_scenario_low"} percent of proxy draws fall below GFI's lowest scenario. GFI's lowest and central paths imply annual growth of [47]{.claim data-claim="proxy_india_2030_gfi_india_implied_growth_low"} and [57]{.claim data-claim="proxy_india_2030_gfi_india_implied_growth_central"} percent, against [34]{.claim data-claim="proxy_india_2030_fastest_draw_growth_max"} percent for the fastest proxy draw. GFI does not publish its model inputs. The disagreement cannot yet be assigned to a single assumption.

Vacancy growth may include income growth; a sensitivity that subtracts the income contribution from the high drift bound gives an India [2035]{.allow-number data-number-kind="year"} middle value of [215]{.claim data-claim="proxy_india_2035_market_value_drift_overlap_adjusted_p50"} million dollars. Pakistan's four multipliers may also overlap; removing its relative urban share and animal protein factors gives a [2035]{.allow-number data-number-kind="year"} range of [2.6]{.claim data-claim="proxy_pakistan_2035_market_value_without_urban_animal_p10"} to [25.3]{.claim data-claim="proxy_pakistan_2035_market_value_without_urban_animal_p90"} million dollars.

## Measurement box

This section separates stated willingness, first purchase, repeat purchase after incentives end, and measured reductions in animal-food purchases. Spending cannot distinguish one-time buyers from repeat buyers.

[Bryant et al.](<https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sustainable-food-systems/articles/10.3389/fsufs.2019.00011/full>) ([2019]{.allow-number data-number-kind="year"}) measure hypothetical intentions, including [1024]{.claim data-claim="acquisition_in_d03_sample_n"} Indian respondents. [Arora, Brent and Jaenicke](<https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/sustainability/sustainability-12-04377/article_deploy/sustainability-12-04377.pdf>) ([2020]{.allow-number data-number-kind="year"}) measure stated choices. [Ahsan, Uzair and Ali](<https://ejournal.upi.edu/index.php/AJSEE/article/view/38766>) ([2021]{.allow-number data-number-kind="year"} online; [2022]{.allow-number data-number-kind="year"} issue) measure Pakistani attitudes, with inconsistent acceptance reporting. [Dean et al.](<https://hau.repository.guildhe.ac.uk/id/eprint/17927/>) ([2023]{.allow-number data-number-kind="year"}) measure pooled willingness to try, buy and pay more, including Pakistan.

The search package contains [9]{.claim data-claim="package_p07_study_records"} study records and [29]{.claim data-claim="package_p07_finding_records"} findings, with no India finding. These are sample-specific responses. They establish neither national purchase rates nor observed first purchase, sustained repeat purchase or measured substitution in either country. The scenarios estimate spending; the behavioural outcomes remain unmeasured.
