# Choosing the policy category

Choose the category that describes the instrument's main direct action. There is no strongest category to select across every document, and a category is not a score for policy quality or effectiveness. Keep all eight categories in the frozen codebook.

For this research, my analytical priorities are product/business authorisation (the route to market), safety (the process and hazard requirements), and halal assurance (operational certification and traceability). This is a research judgement about what to examine closely, not a ranking established by market data. Labels/claims and innovation support explain other distinct mechanisms. A funding policy cannot substitute for food approval, and halal assurance does not establish consumer demand.

Use the existing codebook's decision rules:

- Permission or registration to sell/operate: AUTHORISATION.
- Hazards, hygiene, inspection, recall or biosafety: SAFETY.
- Operational halal certification and traceability: HALAL.
- Consumer labels, declarations, claims or claim-specific logos: INFORMATION.
- Funding, research infrastructure or technical scale-up support: INNOVATION.
- Creation of an authority or broad jurisdiction: FRAMEWORK.
- Import/export clearance and border procedures: TRADE.
- Product-specific ingredients, identity or composition limits: COMPOSITION.

Read the purpose, scope and substantive operative provisions. Record a short reason and page/section. If two functions remain equally central, apply the frozen codebook's purpose/first-substantive-chapter tie-break. If the source is insufficient, mark it uncodable with a reason.

These generic explanations do not disclose the agents' answers to the twenty sampled instruments. Author choices remain blank until independently supplied. Requesting advice or accepting the halal translations does not supply those choices or establish an agreement statistic.
