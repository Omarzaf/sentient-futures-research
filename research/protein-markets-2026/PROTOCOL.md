# Protein market estimation and forecast protocol

Human verification pending. This protocol implements the approved brief without changing its acceptance criteria.

## Instrument identity and replication

Use the questionnaire supplied by the author as the elicitation version. Preserve its original bytes and a text extraction in private working storage. Record its checksum, the supplied filename and every adaptation. Archive timestamps alone do not authenticate a version date.

The instrument permits public resources, including existing forecasts, with disclosure of their influence. Its plant-based question measures retail sales in constant 2025 US dollars. Its cultivated and fermentation questions measure consumer sales through retail and foodservice. Cultivated sales include a pet-food exception. Hybrid products can occur in more than one pillar. Pillar values must not be summed as disjoint market components.

Keep the literal instrument and country adaptations separate from the empirical research estimands. Changing a sales question into a market-share question is an additional analysis, not a faithful replication. Country substitutions do not create an equivalent local GFI series or make the US price deflator a domestic price index. Record local source, channel, currency and resolution changes explicitly. Do not invent Anna's US results or missing model-run settings.

## Matched market denominators

Report the alternative share of a matched market as alternative consumer expenditure divided by matched animal and corresponding alternative consumer expenditure. Also report an explicitly named animal-market penetration ratio when the denominator contains only matched animal products. Keep those two quantities distinct.

Specify product and sales-channel cells before aggregation. Human-food headline shares exclude pet food. Retail-plus-foodservice numerators cannot be divided by retail-only denominators. Retail prices, manufacturer revenues, import values and household expenditure are separate measures. Overlapping pillars require product-level attribution; they cannot be added to construct a total.

Survey-year expenditure is not automatically a 2026 estimate. Record price adjustments, population scaling and any nowcast separately. Apply IMF population scaling once. Preserve rural and urban estimates and expenditure groups, survey weights, strata and PSUs.

## Official reproduction gate

The India target is HCES 2023-24 protein of 61.8 grams per person per day in rural areas and 63.4 in urban areas, each within 0.5 grams. The Pakistan target is HIES 2024-25 chicken of 0.34 kilograms per person per month within published rounding. These are acceptance targets supplied in the brief, not new research estimates.

The India target is the unadjusted estimand: Report594 Table3.18R/U explicitly separates it from meal-adjusted61.2/62.9. Do not add a meal-adjustment prerequisite to this unadjusted target or compare adjusted results against it. The current scripts are incomplete component diagnostics and deliberately return HOLD; that status is not a dynamic acceptance test or evidence that all needed input files are absent. A complete estimator and source/design checks must precede any future gate evaluator.

Both must be reproduced from official unit-level data using the official universe, recall periods, item mapping and weights. Document the official survey strata and PSU fields. A value copied from a published table, an unverified aggregate receipt or a cost-grouping variable called a stratum cannot establish microdata reproduction or design-based uncertainty. Preserve official source and code hashes and the output-row identities.

Until both pass, hold all new market, elasticity, consumption-decomposition and projection results. Independent source collection, protocol work and the official reproductions may continue. Portal registration, payment, communications and publication retain the author's gates.

## Demand and social drivers

Estimate income and own-price relationships for meat, poultry, eggs, dairy and pulses by country and rural or urban residence. Document zero purchases, price identification, unit-value quality, endogeneity and the budget definition. Standard errors must follow the inspected survey design.

For NFHS-5, specify the respondent universe and weekly-consumption outcome, use appropriate weights and design, and report associations or an explicitly defined decomposition with confidence intervals. Do not call religion, caste or residence associations causal effects. Name the Pakistani datasets actually checked and preserve noncomparability or nonavailability.

## Market reconciliation and prices

Keep top-down estimates, bottom-up product or company evidence and HS 2106.10 or 3504.00 imports side by side. These HS categories are not automatically pillar-specific consumer demand. A defensible classification and an explicit treatment of domestic production and re-exports are required. Preserve disagreements greater than twofold rather than averaging them.

The retail table requires named outlets, capture dates, package quantities, protein evidence and a reproducible price-per-gram calculation. Record product availability and capture coverage; a set of observed listings is not a census of every product on sale.

## Forecast and policy requirements

Commit at least ten separately resolvable forecast questions before any projection, including test executions that calculate projections. Quantiles are not additional questions. Preserve exact resolution sources, dates and rules for missing future evidence. The replicated questionnaire does not replace this requirement.

Keep elicited forecasts separate from independently fitted reference-class or Bass results. Report each pillar, country and horizon with P10, P50 and P90 and a documented uncertainty interpretation. Scenario endpoints cannot silently become calibrated predictive quantiles. Sensitivity analysis identifies three influential inputs per projection.

Freeze the policy codebook and instrument sample before independent author coding. The author must code twenty instruments; agents cannot supply that human coding. Report Cohen's kappa and its calculation. No agreement threshold is met until the actual comparison reaches 0.7. Cover every named Indian and Pakistani jurisdiction in the brief. Religious positions require original named and dated authorities and translation notes, with unresolved dating or translation retained.

## Completion

The final opening answers the five research questions within two pages. Every numerical finding has a claims-ledger row and a source-to-output chain. Rebuild every table from raw inputs with one command; provide lawful acquisition instructions for restricted inputs. Include the household-meal thesis as an agenda of proposed tests. Exclude the standalone supply benchmark and news-framing annotation from the main text.

Completion is audited against the brief itself as well as the software verifier. Missing, blocked, failed and unverified requirements are never promoted by a green structural check.
