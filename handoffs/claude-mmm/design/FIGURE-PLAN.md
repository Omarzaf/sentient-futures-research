Plan against all thirteen sections. A section gets an additional figure only where a real sequence, comparison, hierarchy, proportion or distribution can be represented from the available inputs. A plain-prose section can explicitly have no figure; do not manufacture a dataset to satisfy a visual quota.

Each diagram needs an accessible title, textual description, source/section pointer, explicit legend, and a caption stating what its geometry means and does not mean. Distances, point sizes, orbital positions and line lengths must not suggest an unmeasured magnitude. A categorical layout should say that spacing is for readability, not a metric.

Use the register's actual source keys for clickable audit points. Point links should reveal the matching local evidence row, with clear documentary status and the original source URL. Do not try to open raw capture paths in the audit metadata: those private source bytes are intentionally absent. Country cells may link to the relevant existing appendix passage rather than requiring a new ledger.

For the audit figure, 95 is the canonical record count and five aliases are not extra sources. Sixty canonical records are cited in the paper. One of the 63 qualified records supplies bibliographic index metadata only. These are different populations and must remain distinct.
