## Umbrella question:

How could AI reshape the food system transition over the next 10–20 years, and where should the talent and resources be directed today to achieve the best possible outcome?

## Suggested roles

- **Anna: Focus on (AI-accelerated) adoption and displacement**
  - Research (desk research, potential expert surveys) + scenario modeling; together with Umar
  - Sub-question (updated): What will future demand for alternative proteins look like, and what are the key limiting or accelerating factors — including the role of AI — in that trajectory?
  - Method for the core forecast: literature-based driver mapping, combined with structured forecast elicitation from multiple LLMs (used as a proxy for a human superforecaster panel) to generate probability estimates and rationales for alt-protein demand trajectories.
  - Additional bridging component: a short "implied displacement" section translating the alt-protein demand forecast into a plausible range of effects on animal protein demand, using historical substitution analogues (e.g. margarine/butter, plant milk/dairy) and existing literature-based elasticity estimates — framed as a hypothesis for follow-up work, not a validated forecast.
- **Rufaro: Focus on Global North vs South; post-transition scenarios**
  - Global North vs South: how can policy be locally adapted to prevent impacts of unequal distribution as a key sub-question → a follow-up to Umar's work on policy recommendations
  - African food system typologies and animal-dependent livelihoods affected by the protein transition → possibly narrow down geography
  - Implications for animals, farmers, workers and consumers → as a follow-up to Anna's question (what happens after displacement)
- **Umar: Focus on policy, resource and talent allocation**
  - Lead execution of foresight/scenario-planning methodologies
  - Research on AI developments relevant to food systems (alt proteins vs animal agriculture) → in conjunction with Anna
  - Mapping out talent capacity needs (industry, research, policy, advocacy), and policy implications (where to allocate resources) as a key sub-question

## 10-week structure

| Weeks | Focus | Main output |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| 1–2 | Scoping & methodology | Research question, scope, methodology |
| 3–5 | Parallel research | Findings from the three workstreams |
| 5–7 | Synthesis & scenarios | 3–4 plausible future scenarios |
| 7–9 | Strategic implications | Animal-welfare, talent, funding and research priorities |
| 9–10 | Writing & finalization | Final report + executive summary |
