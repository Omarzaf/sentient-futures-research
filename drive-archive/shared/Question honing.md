## Initial sub-question (DEPRECATED)

- Will alternative proteins meaningfully reduce animal product demand as a key sub-question → needs to distinguish between projections of conventional meat market growth (many such present; are alt proteins taken into account?) and projections of consumer demand

### Process of honing the sub-question

Reasoning

- Is it feasible to research in 10 weeks whether alt proteins will meaningfully displace demand for animal proteins in the future? My hunch is no - there are so many factors that might affect both
- So I propose narrowing the scope to: what will future demand for alt proteins look like, and what are the key limiting or accelerating factors in that.
- But still need to consider: how does AI fit in here?
- Then in future potentially beyond the incubator I could expand the project to looking at expected effects on animal protein consumption

Updated approach

- Core deliverable stays as mainly a forecast of alt-protein demand only (adoption curve, drivers, AI-acceleration factors)
  - This stays the same, it's part of the main question not affected by changing this sub-question
- Additional part on how this could affect demand for animal protein: a short, "implied displacement" section that translates your demand forecast into a range, not a point estimate. This would most likely take the form of an all-else-equal scenario where the only thing that changes is alt protein consumption, otherwise it'll turn into an exercise forecasting exogenous factors like demographic change.
  - Method: pick 2-3 historical substitution analogues and pull their known displacement ratios:
    - Margarine vs. butter (how much butter demand actually fell as margarine grew)
    - Plant milk vs. dairy milk (market share shift over the comparable adoption period)
    - Any literature-stated cross-price elasticity or substitution-rate figures for meat vs. plant-based proteins (even rough ones from existing market research/academic papers count)
  - Apply those historical ratios to the alt-protein forecast to get a plausible range: e.g. "if analogous substitution patterns hold, X% alt-protein market share by 2035 could imply somewhere between Y–Z% reduction in animal protein demand"
- Explicitly flag the caveats that make this a bridging estimate, not a forecast itself: historical analogues may not transfer (different product categories, different cultural/psychological substitutability, different starting bases).
- Potential future avenues of this research:
  - Forecast the displacement effect directly
  - Use human superforecasters
