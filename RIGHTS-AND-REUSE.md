# Rights and reuse

This repository is prepared for reading and feedback. No blanket open-content or software license has been selected. Public accessibility, if enabled, does not by itself grant permission to reuse every item.

Third-party publications, datasets, trademarks, and cited material retain their own rights and terms. Follow the original source links and check the applicable terms before reproducing or adapting them. Downloaded third-party papers and program documents are not bundled.

The comparative data provenance records the source FAO table as CC BY 4.0. That statement is retained from the original research artifact and was not independently re-verified during packaging. Preserve the FAO attribution, table/page locators, stated calculations, and limitations when working with these extracted values.

The repository edition omits third-party illustrative photographs and raster map backgrounds. Their original source records remain in the bibliography for provenance. The retained charts, coordinate grids, and location markers show the report's extracted values and approximate locators; they do not establish geographic boundaries.

The Office originals of the orientation reports, the Word and PDF renderings of the India–Pakistan brief, unpublished peer drafts, mentor comments, private meeting notes, personal application materials, credentials, and working downloads are excluded. The brief's Markdown text in this repository is canonical and carries the same content as those renderings. This repository does not grant permission to redistribute those separate materials.

The supervisor report has a separate, exact-file exception: `research/india-pakistan/report/report.pdf` and `research/india-pakistan/report/report.docx` are authored renderings of its canonical [Markdown report](research/india-pakistan/report/REPORT.md). The [reviewed export registry](provenance/supervisor-reviewed-exports.json) binds their hashes to the Markdown, extracted body text and local export-parity evidence. This exception does not admit downloaded publisher PDFs, other Word files, vendor directories or private source material. It grants no blanket reuse license and does not change the pending human scholarly review.

The supervisor edition retains source-specific attribution for FAOSTAT and geoBoundaries under their stated CC BY 4.0 terms. Preserve the source ledger, dataset vintage, boundary caveats and derived-estimate labels alongside those materials. The report and edition do not redistribute full news articles or raw downloads with unresolved reuse terms.

The shareable edition is the clean file tree generated and checked by the repository tools. Historical Git objects, private archive branches, bundles, local workspaces, and pull-request attachments are outside that shareable edition unless they have been separately sanitized and reviewed.

Before substantial republication of the authored drafts, agree attribution and licensing with the maintainer and complete human verification of the claims being reused.
