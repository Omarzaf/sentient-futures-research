# Rights and reuse

This repository is prepared for reading and feedback. No blanket open-content or software license has been selected. Public accessibility, if enabled, does not by itself grant permission to reuse every item.

Third-party publications, datasets, trademarks, and cited material retain their own rights and terms. Follow the original source links and check the applicable terms before reproducing or adapting them. Downloaded third-party papers and program documents are not bundled.

The comparative data provenance records the source FAO table as CC BY 4.0. That statement is retained from the original research artifact and was not independently re-verified during packaging. Preserve the FAO attribution, table/page locators, stated calculations, and limitations when working with these extracted values.

The repository edition omits third-party illustrative photographs and raster map backgrounds. Their original source records remain in the bibliography for provenance. The retained charts, coordinate grids, and location markers show the report's extracted values and approximate locators; they do not establish geographic boundaries.

The Office originals of the orientation reports, unpublished peer drafts, mentor comments, private meeting notes, personal application materials, credentials, and working downloads are excluded. This repository does not grant permission to redistribute those separate materials.

Before substantial republication of the authored drafts, agree attribution and licensing with the maintainer and complete human verification of the claims being reused.
